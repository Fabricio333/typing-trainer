Is Typing Trainer free?

yes. free and open source under the MIT licence.

Where does Typing Trainer store my data?

nowhere. it stays in your browser and can be exported as JSON.

Does Typing Trainer work offline?

yes. after the first visit, the whole app works offline.

Which languages and keyboard layouts does Typing Trainer support?
